Methodology (read this before trusting any number)

How predictions are calculated — fully deterministic, no randomness
  1. Your per-troop stats come from the base attack/defense/HP lookup table for your chosen troop type and tier (T1–T17), each multiplied by your Attack/Defense/HP buff percentages.
  2. The monster's stats come from the monster database (every entry has an explicit Attack, Defense, HP and troop count). Your Boss Attack/Defense Debuff % inputs reduce the monster's attack/defense directly. The monster's HP is never reduced by a debuff — this matches the reference engine, which has no HP-debuff mechanism.
  3. A troop modifier is looked up for the matchup: world bosses and alliance bosses use their own modifier tables, common monsters use a simple tier/troop-type rule, other bosses use a tier-vs-boss modifier table, and everything else falls back to the monster's own per-troop-type modifiers.
  4. Damage per troop = Your Attack × Troop Modifier, then attenuated by an attack-vs-defense contest factor: Your Attack ÷ (Your Attack + Monster Defense).
  5. Recommended Minimum March = the troop count whose total damage output kills the monster's entire HP pool (Monster HP × Monster Troop Count) in one exchange — a closed-form calculation, not a search. Victory is called when your march size meets or exceeds that number.
  6. Estimated Wounded = Monster Attack × Monster Troop Count ÷ Your Per-Troop HP, capped at your march size, on a Victory. A Defeat is recorded as a full loss.
  7. Confidence is a plain read of how much safety margin the result has: Very High (≈0% wounded) down to None (Defeat).

Honesty note: This model is ported from the community-maintained Game-EvonyTKR Monster Simulator (see METHODOLOGY.md in the project repository), with the monster database and every lookup table sourced from data.js. Evony does not publish its internal combat formula, so treat this as a documented, reproducible approximation of the community-maintained reference implementation — not a leak of real game data. When your real result disagrees with the prediction, log it below.
